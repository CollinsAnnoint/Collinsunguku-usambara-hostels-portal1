More About Me

I was once exactly where you are — a student carrying my own bucket to the water point, studying by torchlight when the power went out, and walking these same corridors. I have lived the highs and the lows of hostel life. That is why I do not just manage these blocks — I feel them. Every report you send is not paperwork to me; it is a promise I made to you the day I took this role.

I believe that a hostel is not bricks and bunk beds — it is a family. When your water runs dry, when your light goes out, when you feel unsafe or unheard — that is a broken promise to a comrade, and I take broken promises personally. I built this very system so that your voice reaches me instantly, because silence is the enemy of change.

What drives me is simple: I refuse to watch a comrade suffer in silence when a small act of care could change their whole day. Whether it is fixing a lock at midnight, listening without judging, or standing up for what is fair — I show up. That is not a slogan for me; it is a lifestyle. I lead from the front, not from the office.
